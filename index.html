Key Insight

At the same timestep, each schedule retains a different amount of original signal. Linear steadily reduces signal under the classic DDPM-style β schedule. Cosine preserves structure longer through a smoother curve. Exponential is configured here as a late-collapse schedule: the image stays readable for longer, then rapidly becomes noise near the end. The schedule choice directly shapes what the model sees and learns at each timestep.
